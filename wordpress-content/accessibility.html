Accessibility

Last updated: 27 August 2026

Our approach

Grow aims to make its website usable by as many people as reasonably possible, including people using assistive technologies.

Ongoing work

We aim to use clear structure, keyboard-accessible controls, readable contrast, descriptive links and alternative text for meaningful images. Accessibility is reviewed as content and functionality change.
